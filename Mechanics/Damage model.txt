DAMAGE MODEL  (as implemented in the prototype)
==============================================

This file records how damage actually resolves in code (sim/combat.py,
sim/ballistics.py, sim/weapons.py). The per-weapon and per-enemy stat blocks
elsewhere in mechanics/ are the design targets; the numbers below are what the
running prototype uses, trimmed for readable, decisive fights.


RESOLUTION ORDER
----------------
A hit is resolved shields -> armor -> health:

  1. SHIELDS absorb first. Each weapon carries a shield multiplier:
       - ballistic / heavy      1.0   (neutral)
       - energy (pulse, laser)  1.9   (energy is the anti-shield answer)
       - rail                   0.4   (rail barely dents shields; it is the
                                       anti-armor / anti-flesh answer)
     When a hit would take the shield below zero, the leftover crosses to
     health at the raw (pre-multiplier) rate. Explosive splash also hits
     shields first.

  2. ARMOR is a percentage reduction on the health portion only.
     1 armor point = 1% less damage, per the bible. Rolled per hit inside the
     item's armor band (e.g. a soldier's 18-35). Capped at 80%.

  3. HEALTH is what remains. Zero health kills. Shields do not gate death;
     they are a buffer, not a second health bar.

Health is deliberately a small core. Armor carries the sustained mitigation;
shields are a thin buffer you spend once per engagement.


GLOBAL DAMAGE SCALE
-------------------
weapons.DMG_SCALE = 4.0 multiplies every weapon's rolled damage. The bible's
base figures (pistol 3-4, rifle 3-5) were written for a different pace; at
bible-scale health pools they produce 15-20 second time-to-kill. The scale
brings that into single digits.

Open problem: the bible's per-level Conditioning term inflates health as
characters level. DMG_SCALE will eventually need a matching level term, or
enemies must stay near their base pools.


DIFFICULTY HOOK
---------------
Combatant.incoming_mult scales all damage a character takes (this is the
Difficulty.txt "enemies deal X% damage" lever). Default 1.0. The player is
NOT given a discount - survival is meant to come from armor and shields, not
from a hidden mitigation.


SHIELD REGENERATION
-------------------
Shields regenerate only after a lull with no damage taken, then slowly:

                     delay after last hit    regen rate
  player (Commando)        6 s                3.5 / s      (~20 s to refill)
  enemies                  7 s                2 / s

Once shields break in a fight they stay broken for the rest of it. Regen is
a between-fights recovery, not something you out-heal under fire. (Design
intent from Statsystem.txt is that recharge scales with the Logic stat; the
prototype uses the fixed rates above.)


PROTOTYPE POOLS
---------------
Player (Commando L1, debug starter kit - no loot system yet):
  ~78 health, ~57 shields, 20% armor.
  A bare Commando (Leather 3% / ~17 shields) folds in about a second of fire.

Enemies (sim/combat.py ENEMY_PROFILES) - human factions run thin:
  Imperium soldier        70 hp / 14 sh / 18-35% armor
  Imperium squad leader   95 hp / 14 sh / 18-35%
  Pirate                  75 hp / 40 sh / 11-23%
  Scavenger               35 hp /  0 sh /  0-11%
  Consortium drone        50 hp / 200 sh / 20%   (shield archetype - unchanged)
  Consortium controller   80 hp / 360 sh / 20%   (shield archetype - unchanged)

The Consortium keeps its large shield pools on purpose: that archetype exists
to force the player onto energy weapons.


TIME-TO-KILL TARGETS
--------------------
Point blank, every round hitting (the worst case; real fights run 2-4x longer
once range spread and cover are involved):

                    scavenger   soldier    shield tank (drone)
  pistol              ~1.1 s     ~3.6 s      ~9.8 s  (near useless)
  combat rifle burst  <1 s       ~1.2 s      ~2.8 s
  combat shotgun      1 shell    ~1.0 s      ~4.0 s
  rail rifle          1 shot     ~1.0 s      ~6.0 s
  pulse carbine       ~0.5 s     ~1.1 s      ~2.6 s  (best vs shields)
  rocket launcher     1 shot     1 shot      1 shot

  guard combat rifle -> naked player   ~0.6 s
  guard combat rifle -> geared player  ~1.7 s

Pistols are a backup that armor shrugs off. Naked targets drop almost
instantly. Shields and armor are what let anything trade fire at all.
