Interacting with items:

- Holding ALT-key or mouse-overing will edge-highlight any nearby lootable containers, interactable items / consoles and any dead enemies.
- Left-clicking will interact with mouse-overed target.

Loot containers:

Chest:
	May contain credits, ammunition, medical supplies, weapons, armor, modules.
Locker:
	May contain ammunition, medical supplies and weapons, modules, armor.
Safe:
	May contain ammunition or credits.
Enemies:
	Everything, depending from enemy type.
