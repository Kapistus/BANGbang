Background:
	Wastelands surround more or less civilized areas of the new world.
	It is often dangerous to venture into the wastes alone, or even in groups,
	as they are full of mutants, beasts, radiation hotspots and other hazards.
	Nevertheless, those willing to risk their lives may find secrets and valuable
	equipment among the ruined remnants of the old world.
