Consortium:
	People who want to unite flesh and circuit. Obsessive over technology and biological material.
	- High shields, low health, medium armor.
	- Employ energy-based weapons.
Imperium:
	
	- Well-trained soldiers with good training and equipment.
	- Balanced shields, health and armor.
Mutants:
	Degenerates, freaks of nature, irradiated people.
	- Medium health, regenerate health. No shields, low to medium armor.
Pirates:
	Outlaws. Attack lone vessels and leave no survivors while taking anything worth a credit with them.
	- Medium health, low to medium shields, low to high armor.
	
Scavengers:
	"Scavs" pick up anything worth hauling with them, some are violent, while others are merely trying to survive.
	- Medium health, low shields, low to medium armor.


IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
--------------------
Prototype enemy stats (sim/combat.py ENEMY_PROFILES): human factions
(Imperium, Pirates, Scavengers) run on trimmed health and shield pools so
fights are decisive - see each unit file and Mechanics/Damage model.txt.
The Consortium keeps its large shield pools (drone 200, controller 360) on
purpose: that archetype exists to push the player onto energy weapons, which
carry a 1.9x shield multiplier.

Enemy behaviour (sim/ai.py) is a four-mode state machine - see
Enemies/Behaviour.txt.
