ENEMY BEHAVIOUR  (as implemented - sim/ai.py)
=============================================

Every guard runs a four-mode state machine: IDLE, PATROL, SEARCH, COMBAT.
The guard does not snap between modes. A single "alert" value in 0..1 drives
which mode is active and also scales movement speed, turn rate and how far
the head sweeps, so transitions read as gradual.


ALERT
-----
alert rises on stimulus and decays otherwise:
  - identified the player in the narrow cone   fast rise
  - player in peripheral vision                medium rise
  - heard a player sound                       slow rise, scaled by loudness
  - nothing seen or heard                      decay

Mode is chosen from alert with a hysteresis dead band, so a guard winding
down from a chase eases PATROL <- ... <- SEARCH rather than cutting straight
back to patrol.


MODES
-----
IDLE     Standing still, sweeping its head. PATROL drops into this for a few
         seconds at random points along the route, then carries on - guards
         are not always walking.

PATROL   Walks its waypoint loop. Pauses briefly at each waypoint.

SEARCH   Moves to its best guess of where the stimulus was and pokes around
         nearby, scanning, for a while. The guard is NEVER told the true
         source of a sound - it hears a bearing and a loudness and walks a
         guess along that bearing (louder = closer), so it can be baited.
         Losing sight of the player drops the guard here, not straight to
         patrol, and it keeps hunting for about 25 seconds before giving up.
         Any fresh contact resets that timer.

COMBAT  Holds a firing distance from the player (advances if too far, backs
         off if too close), fires its weapon when it has line of sight.
         Reloads between fights. Loses sight -> SEARCH at the last known
         position with alert still high.


PERCEPTION
----------
Sight is a single line-of-sight ray plus a cone angle/range test (identify
cone is narrow and long; peripheral is wide and short). Hearing samples the
same sound-propagation field the player's own hearing uses, so a guard only
reacts to a noise once the wavefront actually reaches it, and only if enough
energy survives the trip.


TUNING NUMBERS (sim/ai.py, subject to change)
  view range 17 m; identify cone 40 deg half-angle to 15 m; peripheral cone
  95 deg half-angle to 7 m
  base speed from the enemy profile (~0.85 m/s), up to ~1.9x at full alert
  search hold ~26 s after last contact
