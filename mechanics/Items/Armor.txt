Listed protective gear represent base values.
Items found in-game may include variables.

armor value: The amount of protection the item provides, 1 point of armor = 1% of damage reduction
	     against hits taken.

vision penalty: Reduces line of sight to the sides and back.
	       poor peripheral vision: no line of sight to the back or sides after 45-degrees each direction.
	       none: no line of sight to the back.
	       enhanced peripheral vision: ocular video feedback showing surrounding environment.
movement speed penalty: Reduces movement speed.
requirements: Required to wear.
Module slots: Some items have room for upgrades and modules. Each item has 0-3 module slots.
