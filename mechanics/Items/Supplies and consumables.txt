Listed supplies represent base values.
Items found in-game may include variables.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
-----------------
Medical supplies:
-----------------
Properties:

Bandages: 
	Restores ( target_missing_health / ( intelligence * ( level / 2 ) ) health to target. Cannot be used in combat.
	Gives target "Treated"* for 2 minutes.
	Weight: 1 kg
Traumapack: 
	Restores ( target_missing_health / ( intelligence * level ) health to target. Cannot be used in combat.
	Gives target "Treated"* for 2 minutes.
	Weight: 1 kg
Adrenaline pack: 
	User gains + 10% movement speed and + 5% armor value for 30 seconds.
	After the effect ends, user gains -10% movement speed for 20 seconds. 
	Weight: 1 kg
Hypo:
	User gains +10% armor, +10% movement speed for 2 minutes, but takes 1% health damage every second for 10 seconds.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
*Treated: Medical equipment cannot be used on this target until Treated wears off.
