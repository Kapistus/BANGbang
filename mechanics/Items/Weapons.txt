Listed weapons represent base values.
Items found in-game may include variables.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE: the running prototype (sim/weapons.py) multiplies every
damage figure below by DMG_SCALE = 4.0 to reach playable time-to-kill against
bible-scale health. The bible values are the design baseline; the ratios
between weapons are preserved. Prototype-only additions:
  - combat rifle and SMG have a full-auto alternate fire mode ("b" to toggle):
    SMG 13 rounds/s, rifle 6 rounds/s, both at reduced accuracy.
  - rocket launcher reloads after every shot.
See mechanics/Mechanics/Weapon mechanics.txt and Damage model.txt.

damage: the amount of damage a single hit deals.
range: effective range of the weapon. Hits beyond the optimal range have significantly increased spread.
accuracy: the amount of shots that make it to the crosshair per unit range.
	( example : 0.9 accuracy gun fired from 1 unit away = 90% + 0.09 = 99%. )
	( example2 : 0.9 accuracy gun fired from 2 unit away = 0.9^2 + 0.09 = 0.9 ratio. )
	( ( accuracy^ range_in_units ) + 0.08 * accuracy )
Moving induces a 10% accuracy penalty.
weight: the weapon's weight in kilograms.
Module slots: Some items have room for upgrades and modules. Each item has 0-3 module slots.
