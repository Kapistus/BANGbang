IMPLEMENTATION NOTE (sim/combat.py):
The prototype runs a leaner Commando than the design baseline - health is a
small core and armor + shields are the survival stats. As built, a level 1
Commando with the debug starter kit (no loot system yet) is roughly:
  ~78 health, ~57 shields, 20% armor, ~4.2 tiles/s.
Shields regenerate 3.5/s but only after a 6 s lull (~20 s to refill). A
stripped Commando (Leather 3% / ~17 shields) dies in about a second of fire.
See mechanics/Mechanics/Damage model.txt.
